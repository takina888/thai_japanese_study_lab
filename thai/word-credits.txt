STATIC OUTLINE GENERATION

Source words: dist/thai-word-content.js
Output: dist/thai/word-tracing-forms.json
Generator: scripts/build-thai-word-tracing.py

Each complete Thai string is shaped by HarfBuzz using the font's OpenType
tables, with monotone grapheme clusters. Its glyph IDs, offsets, and advances
are then passed to fontTools to extract and combine the positioned outlines.
Sara Am decomposition and tone/upper-vowel positioning are handled during
shaping; input Thai spelling is not manually decomposed or reordered.

The viewBox height is 400. The reference consonant height is approximately
200 units; uniform scaling reduces it only when necessary to retain vertical
mark space or fit the maximum width. Width is based on the shaped advance,
normally at least 800 and at most 2000. A one-advance word may use 600.
The painted outline retains at least 80 horizontal and 40 vertical units of
padding. Short words receive additional whitespace, not horizontal stretching.

Each layouts entry includes the exact source text to allow stale-asset
detection. Its normalized segments are nonvisible horizontal practice regions
derived from visible advancing glyphs. Zero-advance upper/lower/tone marks
remain associated with their parent cluster and do not create separate cells.
These regions do not clip the word, represent syllables, or prescribe a pen
order. They must not be displayed as equal-width letter boxes.

Reproduce (Python 3, fontTools, local HarfBuzz shared library, and Node):
python scripts/build-thai-word-tracing.py

Check exact reproduction without writing:
python scripts/build-thai-word-tracing.py --check

Inspect glyphs, mark placement, and practice-region ownership without writing:
python scripts/build-thai-word-tracing.py --check --audit

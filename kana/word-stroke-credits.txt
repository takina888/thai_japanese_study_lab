WORD HANDWRITING: ADDITIONAL HIRAGANA SVGs

File: word-extra-strokes.json
Scope: 34 derived teaching glyphs (20 voiced, 5 semi-voiced, 9 small hiragana).
These are local adaptations, not verbatim downloads of upstream extra glyphs.

ADAPTATIONS

1. Voiced and semi-voiced kana retain each base outline, centerline, clip path,
   and stroke group in its original order. The base forms are not rescaled.
2. Dakuten are two explicit short down-right centerlines with matching clipped
   round-ended outlines. The left dot is drawn before the right dot, after the
   base character. Each dot is an independent stroke.
3. Handakuten are one explicit circular centerline, starting at the bottom
   (six o'clock) and continuing clockwise, after the base character. A clipped
   ring outline has a transparent center. This uses the common one-stroke form;
   a two-stroke brush-writing alternative is not modeled.
4. Small kana reuse the base stroke groups and uniformly scale all their
   outline/centerline geometry to 68%. Translation is (10, 310) in the original
   1024-unit cell, placing them lower-left for horizontal word writing. The
   reveal pen is scaled by the same factor. SVG arc segments may be normalized
   to equivalent cubic approximations by fontTools during this transform.
5. All local SVG IDs and fragment references are namespaced to the resulting
   character's Unicode code point. Each asset is independent and uses no
   external references, images, font loading, scripts, or event attributes.

The new diacritic geometry was constructed locally; it was not extracted from
the educational reference images. The existing ordered centerlines, rather
than an automatic traversal of font outlines, determine base stroke order.
